# What My Pet Thinks — TikTok Strategy Skill File (FINAL)

Last updated: February 17, 2026
Status: LIVE. App deployed, Stripe live ($9.99/mo), ready to post.

---

## Core Principle

Every post makes someone think "I need to see what MY pet would text me." Text conversation format is your lead. The URL (whatmypetthinks.com) and hashtag (#WhatMyPetThinks) are baked into every output image.

---

## The Marketing Frame

**Say:** "Find out what your pet would text you"
**Not:** "AI translates your pet's thoughts"

"My cat texted me" is a hook. "AI caption on pet photo" is a feature description.

---

## Format Priority

### 1. Text Conversation Slideshows (60% of posts)

Each slide is a different pet's iMessage text conversation screenshot. This is your highest-value format:
- People read every bubble (high watch time = algorithm boost)
- Text screenshots are the most shared format on the internet
- Each conversation has built-in escalation (6 messages)
- "My pet texted me" is a hook people can't scroll past

**Slideshow spec:**
- 6 slides exactly
- Portrait: 1024x1536 (9:16)
- Slide 1: hook text on expressive pet photo
- Slides 2-6: text conversation screenshots from the app (with pet photo + branded footer)
- Trending sound (INSTRUMENTAL — people need to READ)
- Max 5 hashtags

### 2. Caption Slideshows (20% of posts)

Classic subtitle-on-photo format for variety. Same slideshow structure, captioned images instead of text conversations.

### 3. Screen Recording Reveals (15% of posts)

Film yourself generating a text conversation and reacting. Or film someone ELSE reading their pet's texts. The text convo format gives you 6 messages to react to instead of one caption — much better for video.

### 4. Duets / Stitches (5% of posts)

Stitch viral pet videos with "I found out what this pet would text its owner." Low effort, once per day max.

## Hook Formula

> [Another person] + [conflict or doubt] → showed them the pet's texts → they reacted

### Top 15 Hooks (Start With These):

1. I found out what my cat would text me and I'm not okay
2. My dog started texting me and it went exactly how you'd think
3. My cat left me on read. MY CAT.
4. I showed my mom what her cat would text her and she lost it
5. Golden retriever texts are EXACTLY what you think they are
6. My dog's texts started sweet and ended in a hostage negotiation about cheese
7. Dog texts vs cat texts — it's not even close
8. I found out what my pet would text me and now I need therapy
9. My cat texted me "no" and nothing else
10. I showed my girlfriend what her cat texts about her behind her back
11. French bulldog texts have more attitude than any human I know
12. My hamster has a texting personality and it's AGGRESSIVE
13. I let every pet in this house text me. Someone needs to be rehomed.
14. My cat sent me a passive aggressive text about dinner being late
15. The way my cat would text me is so accurate it's scary

### The Test:
Before posting: "Who's the other person, and what's the conflict?" No answer → rewrite.

## Slideshow Structure (6 Slides)

### Slide 1: The Hook
- Bold text on expressive pet photo
- White text, black outline or dark semi-transparent bar
- Font: at least 6.5% of image height
- Position: center (NOT top 10% or bottom 20%)
- Max 2 lines
- NO app name on slide 1

### Slides 2-5: Text Conversations
- Each slide = one pet's full text conversation from the app
- Different pets/breeds on each slide
- Order: good → great → amazing
- Mix voice styles (mostly Funny, throw in a Passive Aggressive or Gen-Z)
- Pet photo and branded footer already baked into each image

### Slide 6: Best Conversation + CTA
- Single funniest text conversation
- Text overlay: "Find out what YOUR pet would text you → whatmypetthinks.com #WhatMyPetThinks"

### Caption Template:
```
[2-3 sentence story about the texts]

Find out what your pet would text you → whatmypetthinks.com

#WhatMyPetThinks #TranslateYourPet #[pet type] #[breed] #funny
```

## Screen Recording for Text Convos (20-30 seconds)

**Second 1-3:** Camera on pet. "Let's see what she'd text me."

**Second 4-8:** Screen recording: upload photo, select Text Convo, tap translate, loading.

**Second 9-25:** Conversation appears. Read each message or react to each one. Let camera catch your face as messages 4-5-6 escalate and go off the rails.

**Second 25-30:** Final conversation image on screen. whatmypetthinks.com visible.

**Best variant:** Film someone ELSE reading their pet's texts for the first time.

## Trending Sounds

**Critical for text convos:** People need to READ the bubbles. Choose instrumental sounds only.

Best: lo-fi beats, light instrumental, comedic background music, dramatic classical, trending instrumental loops

Avoid: anything with lyrics that compete with reading

## Content Batching (Weekly — 1-2 hours)

1. **Generate 15+ text conversations** — mix dogs, cats, breeds, voice styles. Rate 1-5. Keep 4s and 5s only.
2. **Generate 10+ captioned images** for secondary posts.
3. **Assemble 7-10 slideshows** — 5 text convo (Templates A, B, C, D), 2-3 caption.
4. **Write hooks and captions** using templates.
5. **Save as TikTok drafts** — full week ready.

### Daily routine (10 minutes):
1. Scroll TikTok 5 min, find trending instrumental sound
2. Open pre-made slideshow
3. Add sound
4. Paste caption
5. Publish
6. Reply to every comment within first hour

## Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. Every slideshow is 6 slides.
2. Every slide 1 has a readable hook.
3. Every image is portrait 9:16.
4. Every post has a trending sound (instrumental for text convo slides).
