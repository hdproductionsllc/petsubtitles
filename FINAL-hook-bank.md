# What My Pet Thinks — Hook Bank & Content Templates (FINAL)

Last updated: February 15, 2026
Status: Text Conversation is the LEAD format. Every hook centers on "my pet texted me."

---

## The Frame

**Say:** "Find out what your pet would text you"
**Never say:** "AI translates your pet's thoughts" / "AI captions" / "pet translator"

Every hook, DM, bio, and CTA uses the texting frame.

---

## Hook Formula

> [Another person] + [conflict or doubt] → showed them the pet's texts → they reacted

## Caption Writing Rules

1. Frame everything around texting. Not "translated" or "captioned." Your pet TEXTED you.
2. Tell a micro-story. "My cat texted me about dinner and things escalated" beats "Try What My Pet Thinks."
3. Write like a text to a friend. Lowercase ok. Fragments ok. 1-2 emojis max.
4. Mention URL naturally. "find out what yours would text you → whatmypetthinks.com"
5. End with engagement bait. "Which conversation was funniest?" or "What would YOUR pet text you?"
6. Under 150 words. Max 5 hashtags.

**Caption template:**
```
[2-3 sentence story about the texts]

Find out what your pet would text you → whatmypetthinks.com

#WhatMyPetThinks #TranslateYourPet #[pet type] #[breed] #funny
```
